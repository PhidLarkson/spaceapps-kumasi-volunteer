# Proud to Volunteer card maker

For volunteers of NASA Space Apps Kumasi 2026 (14 and 15 November, KNUST).

Open https://phidlarkson.github.io/spaceapps-kumasi-volunteer/, choose a photo, fit your face in the red oval, add your name and role, and download your card in post (4:5) or status (9:16) size.

Photos are processed in the browser only. Nothing is uploaded.

This is a locally organised event held under the NASA Space Apps Challenge programme. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by NASA.
